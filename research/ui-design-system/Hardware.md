# Hardware

Desktop box 300 mm wide, 200 mm deep, 120 mm tall. With the tilted screen it is 149 mm tall. All panels 3 mm laser-cut acrylic with finger joints; the front panel is acrylic or warm plywood.

## Front face, left to right, one row at mid height

| Part | Size | Label | Center from left |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Green arcade button, lit | 30 mm | APPROVE / 批准 | 40 mm |
| Red arcade button, lit | 30 mm | REJECT / 拒绝 | 90 mm |
| Mushroom kill switch, latching, yellow collar | 40 mm head, 50 mm collar | KILL / 冻结 | 150 mm |
| Metal push-to-talk | 16 mm | TALK / 说话 | 212 mm |
| Toggle switch | standard | EN / 中文 | 258 mm |

A speaker grille of small holes sits low on the front right. Buttons sit about 64 mm above the base.

## Top lid

- Top left: 5 inch landscape screen, 800x480, in a 3D-printed bezel, tilted back about 15 degrees from the lid. Assumption: the tilt is measured from the lid plane, which makes a low wedge about 29 mm high at the rear.
- Top right: four 5 mm LEDs at 30 mm pitch, labeled underneath COORD, AGENT 1, AGENT 2, GUARD.

## Inside

Raspberry Pi 5 with active cooler at the rear left on standoffs. Small perfboard in the middle with a ULN2003 driver and resistors, labeled wires. USB speaker behind the front grille. Vent slots low on the sides and high on the rear. No battery.

## Rear wall

Cutouts for 2 micro HDMI cables and 1 USB-C power cable.

## Around the console

15.6 inch slim portable monitor on its own stand behind it. Black dynamic USB microphone on a short stand to the left.
